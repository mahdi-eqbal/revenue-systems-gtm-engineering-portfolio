Dublin Painting Demo privacy notice

Updated 15 November 2026.

Mahdi Eqbal operates this portfolio demonstration. Please use synthetic test details only.

What the form collects

The Meta Instant Form collects a name and email address. Meta receives submissions, and the connected HighLevel CRM receives a copy for the demo.

How submissions are used

The operator uses the submitted details to verify that form data reaches HighLevel and that the fields are mapped correctly. This demo does not use submissions for marketing outreach. The operator intends to remove test records from the demo after verifying delivery. Meta and HighLevel may retain data according to their own policies.

Contact

For questions about this demo or to request removal of a submission from the demo CRM, contact Mahdi Eqbal through the Dublin Painting Demo Facebook Page.
