What this prototype demonstrates

EVENTS

Clean intake

Checks required fields, allowed event types, variants, and timestamps before accepting a normalized campaign outcome.

LEARNING

Careful comparison

Reports variant outcomes using unique delivered prospects and flags small samples as directional only.

GOVERNANCE

Review before action

Illustrative account-fit logic exposes missing evidence, produces draft-only sample copy, and keeps outreach disabled.
