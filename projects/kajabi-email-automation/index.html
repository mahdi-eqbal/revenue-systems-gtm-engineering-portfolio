01 / The business goal

Make signup useful without sending unwanted marketing.

A visitor requests the AI Productivity Starter Kit. They should receive access after confirming their address; marketing enrollment should depend on the consent choice. The business wants a clear welcome-and-nurture sequence without manually managing every sign-up.

Capture

Published Kajabi landing page, first name and email, plus an optional unchecked marketing-consent field.

Qualify consent

Double opt-in confirms the address. A published automation checks the recorded consent response before enrolling the subscriber.

Nurture

Tagging and sequence enrollment route opted-in contacts into a three-part educational email series.

03 / Email strategy

Three messages, one learning path.

DAY 0 / Immediately

Welcome & quick wins

Introduces the Starter Kit with three actionable AI prompts and a short checklist.

DAY 2 / Scheduled

Meeting follow-up exercise

Teaches one repeatable AI-assisted process for turning meeting notes into follow-up actions.

DAY 4 / Scheduled

Weekly habit & next step

Shows how to build a light recurring productivity habit and suggests a next action.

The three messages and their cadence were configured and saved in Kajabi. The demonstration focuses on configuration and verified enrollment behavior, not production deliverability analytics.
